# Company Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
- All employees are entitled to 24 days of annual leave per year.
- Leave must be approved by your manager at least 3 days in advance.
- Unused leave can be carried forward up to 5 days only.

## Sick Leave
- Employees get 12 days of sick leave per year.
- Medical certificate required for leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.
- Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

## Work From Home Policy
- Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week.
- Prior approval from manager required.
- Must be available during core hours: 10 AM to 4 PM.

## Public Holidays
- Company observes 10 public holidays per year.
- Holiday list is published at the start of each year.
